About Angola

Angola is a Southern African country rich in natural resources, cultural diversity, and history. It features vast landscapes, from Atlantic coastlines to inland plateaus, and plays an important role in regional economic and political dynamics.

The President

The President of Angola is both the Head of State and Government, responsible for executive power, national policy direction, and international representation.

Learn More

The Government

Angola operates under a presidential system. The government is composed of executive, legislative, and judicial branches, ensuring governance, law-making, and justice administration.

History

Angola's history spans pre-colonial kingdoms, Portuguese colonization, independence in 1975, and a long civil war that ended in 2002, shaping the modern nation.

Economics

Angola's economy is driven largely by oil and gas exports, with growing diversification in agriculture, mining, and infrastructure development.

Education

The education system in Angola continues to expand, focusing on improving access, literacy, and higher education to support national development.

National Symbols

Angola’s national symbols include the flag, emblem, and anthem, representing the country's history, independence struggle, and national identity.
