#{msg.aboutAngola}

Angola is a country on the west-central coast of Africa. It is the second-largest Lusophone country and the seventh-largest country in Africa. Known for its vast natural resources, including oil and diamonds, Angola is a nation of incredible cultural diversity and stunning landscapes.

O Presidente

Since 2017, João Lourenço has served as the President of Angola. His administration focuses on economic diversification, fighting corruption, and strengthening international relations to position Angola as a regional leader in Southern Africa.

#{msg.government}

The Government of Angola operates under a presidential system where the President is both Head of State and Head of Government. The executive branch is supported by a Council of Ministers that oversees the development and implementation of national policies.

Our history

From the ancient kingdoms like the Kingdom of Kongo to the colonial era and the struggle for independence achieved in 1975, Angola's history is a testament to resilience. Today, the nation celebrates its peace and reconstruction efforts since 2002.

Economia

Angola is one of Africa’s fastest-growing economies, heavily centered on the petroleum sector, with emerging growth in agriculture, mining, and telecommunications.

#{msg.education}

Investment in human capital is a priority, with the government expanding access to primary, secondary, and higher education across all 18 provinces.

National Symbols

The Flag (Red, Black, and Gold), the National Anthem "Angola Avante!", and the Coat of Arms represent the blood shed for independence and the wealth of the land.
